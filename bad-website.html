Question 2

Bad Website Analysis

Ten reasons the design of Arngren.net can make browsing difficult.

Website selected: Arngren.net

Arngren is an active website offering many different products. I chose it as an example of a site with a crowded design that can make information harder to find. These points are design opinions based on its publicly accessible homepage, not claims about its products or business practices.

Visit the Bad Website ↗

10 reasons why the design is bad

  1. Too much clutter: Many products and links appear close together, making the page overwhelming.
  2. Weak visual hierarchy: It is difficult to tell which information is most important.
  3. Inconsistent image sizes: Product pictures vary in size and are not organized consistently.
  4. Very little whitespace: Items are packed tightly together without enough room to breathe.
  5. Confusing navigation: Categories and links blend into the rest of the information.
  6. Inconsistent text formatting: Multiple text treatments make the page harder to scan.
  7. Hard to compare products: Different items and prices are scattered around the page.
  8. Limited grouping: Related products are not always presented in clear sections.
  9. Distracting overall appearance: So many competing visual elements draw attention in different directions.
  10. Difficult first impression: A new visitor may struggle to know where to begin looking.

How it could be improved

A simpler layout with clear headings, consistent product cards, product categories, and more spacing would make the site easier to use.
